Generate your admin password hash

Type your real admin password below. Your browser computes the bcrypt hash locally — the password itself is never sent anywhere, not even to this page's own server. Paste the resulting hash into Render as ADMIN_PASSWORD_HASH.

This hash is one-way — it can confirm your password later but can't be reversed back into it. If you ever want to change your password, just come back to this page and generate a new hash.
